Eight properties are in the running for the county’s next jail

Land proposals were due Sept. 28. Eight were submitted: three in Caribou, two in Houlton, and one each in Presque Isle, Limestone and Fort Fairfield. Presque Isle’s city council chose not to offer city-owned land; a privately owned Presque Isle parcel is among the eight. The county’s architect, SMRT, can evaluate up to five sites. A consultant last year put construction at $91 to 112 million, and a bond question could go to voters as early as next year.

The county has not yet posted the list of sites. Reporting: Bangor Daily News, Oct. 5 · The County, Oct. 5 · Deadline: Bangor Daily News, Sept. 10

Corrections keeps the rule allowing sanctions regardless of a hearing’s outcome

In its response to public comments on proposed changes to its adult resident discipline rule, the Maine Department of Corrections said Procedure O.21 “will not be removed.” The provision lets staff restrict privileges or take other action whether or not a disciplinary case is brought, and states: “A dismissal or a finding of not guilty does not preclude taking any such action.” Comments closed Aug. 21.

Rule text: Policy 20.1, Procedure O.21, p. 37 (maine.gov) · Reporting: Maine Morning Star, Sept. 28 · Bangor Daily News, Sept. 28

State prisons held 2,100 people on an average day in August

The Department of Corrections’ August report puts the average incarcerated population at 2,100: 1,884 men and 215 women. The average for 2026 so far is 2,095.

Corrections’ rules on legislator access to facilities are due

LD 1962 requires the commissioner to adopt rules setting which areas legislators may enter, how they may speak with residents and staff, and when entry may be refused. The same law lets the Senate President and House Speaker fill a vacant Board of Visitors seat if the Governor has not filled it within 90 days. It became law without the Governor’s signature on April 19, 2026, and took effect July 29.

Bill history: LD 1962, 132nd Legislature (legislature.maine.gov)
