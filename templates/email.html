CorrectionOCT 07

Aroostook has not closed its jail-site search

An earlier version of this issue said county commissioners ended the search on Oct. 1. They did not; the search is open and eight properties were submitted. It also listed LD 1962 and the $4 million jail allocation as this week’s news. Both happened in April: LD 1962 became law without the Governor’s signature on April 19, 2026, and the $4 million was a one-time 2025–26 allocation signed April 16, 2026.

Aroostook CountyOCT 05

Eight properties are in the running for the county’s next jail

Land proposals were due Sept. 28. Eight were submitted: three in Caribou, two in Houlton, and one each in Presque Isle, Limestone and Fort Fairfield. SMRT can evaluate up to five. A consultant last year put construction at $91 to 112 million; a bond question could go to voters as early as next year. The county has not yet posted the list; reporting: Bangor Daily News.

StatewideSEP 23

Corrections keeps the rule allowing sanctions regardless of a hearing’s outcome

Responding to public comments on its adult resident discipline rule, the Department of Corrections said Procedure O.21 “will not be removed.” The provision states: “A dismissal or a finding of not guilty does not preclude taking any such action.” Rule text, p. 37.
