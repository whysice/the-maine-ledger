DOCTalk Archive Log: Windham Women and Aroostook County Jail

The Maine DOC staff newsletter (80 issues, 1997 to 2017) names Aroostook County Jail in only four issues, but it gives a dated record of where women were held at Windham, who ran those units, and incidents that can anchor records requests. The archive stops in spring 2017, so nothing here describes current conditions.

06Records to request

The newsletter names these documents but does not print them. Check the MDOC website first for the PREA reports, since federal PREA rules require final audit reports to be published.

  • Aroostook County Jail inspection reports from MDOC’s Inspections Division, 2004 onward. The 2015 article says each jail is audited at least every two years.
  • Records behind the 2013 entry “Assisted Aroostook County with Overcrowding Needs” and the 2014 bunk project: who asked, what was agreed, how many beds were added.
  • The County and Municipal Detention Advisory Committee’s 2015 member list, minutes and any revised jail standards.
  • PREA audit reports for MCC and the Southern Maine Re-entry Center from August 2014, and each later cycle.
  • ACA audit reports for MCC from 2005, 2008, 2011 and 2014, including the two standards failed for building age and any waiver requests.
  • National Institute of Corrections security audit reports for MCC from 2012 and 2015, with the corrective action plans.
  • Incident reports for the dated suicide attempts, the 2015 “9/18 incident”, the 2015 prisoner deaths, and the 2016 disturbance and hostage situation at MCC.
  • Yearly counts from MCC’s Significant Incident Reporting Database.
  • The 2012 contract between DOC and York County Jail for the Alfred center.
  • The MCC feasibility study by Pulitzer, Bogard & Associates and SMRT Architects, due to the Criminal Justice and Public Safety Committee on February 1, 2014.
  • Dr. Patricia Van Voorhis’s 2005 evaluation of the Women’s Unit and Dr. Patricia Hardyman’s 2015 review of classification for women.
  • “Maine’s Women Offenders: What Do We Know?” by Erica Hansen King, June 17, 2008, from the Muskie School and MDOC.

07Limits

Every issue was read through a tool that paraphrases the PDF text, so pull exact wording and page numbers from the PDF before quoting. Rows marked Yes were checked a second time against the issue; all other rows rest on one read.

  • Not read: May/Jun 2015 would not load. The archive’s May/Jun 2004 file is a duplicate of Jan/Feb 2004.
  • Cut off partway: Nov/Dec 2012 after p. 43, Nov/Dec 2013 after about p. 37, Nov/Dec 2014 after about p. 36, Sep/Oct 2015 after about p. 39, and Nov/Dec 2015 after about p. 37. The last one loses the back page on the women’s center groundbreaking.
  • Archive gaps: no issues for 2000 to 2002 or 2007, only five items before 2003, and nothing after Mar/Apr 2017.
  • Jul/Aug 2008 reprints most of May/Jun 2008, so items in pages 5 to 25 of that issue date to May and June.
  • The source disagrees with itself in places: the Alfred center is sometimes printed as “located in York”; the women’s center groundbreaking is a 2015 item in one issue and “initiated in 2016” in another; MCC’s 2014 ACA result is given as both 99.5 overall and 99.5% of non-mandatory standards.
  • Page numbers can be off by one throughout.
