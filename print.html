OCT 05Aroostook County

Eight properties are in the running for the county’s next jail

Land proposals, due Sept. 28: three in Caribou, two in Houlton, one each in Presque Isle, Limestone and Fort Fairfield. SMRT can evaluate up to five. A consultant put construction at $91 to 112 million; a bond vote could come as early as next year. List not yet posted by the county; reported by the Bangor Daily News, Oct. 5.

Corrections keeps the rule allowing sanctions regardless of a hearing’s outcome

Responding to public comments, the department said Procedure O.21 “will not be removed.” It states: “A dismissal or a finding of not guilty does not preclude taking any such action.” Rule text: Policy 20.1, p. 37.
