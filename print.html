OCT 01Aroostook County

County closes its site search for a new jail

Commissioners have ended the search for a site to replace the county jail. Construction estimates run $85 to 120 million, and a bond question could go to voters next year. The county has not yet published a preferred-site recommendation.

SEP 30Statewide

Corrections keeps rule allowing sanctions before a hearing ends

The Department of Corrections has retained a disciplinary rule that permits sanctions to be imposed before a disciplinary hearing concludes — or after a finding of not guilty. The rule stands as adopted; the department has not announced a revision.

OCT 02State House

LD 1962, on access to facilities, is now law

The bill addresses legislator access to correctional facilities and vacancies on county Boards of Visitors. It has been enacted and signed. The Boards, required by statute, have carried unfilled seats in several counties.

OCT 01Counties

Jails to get $4 million more in state funding for 2027

County jails will receive an additional $4 million in state support for 2027. It is the first increase in the state’s share since 2021, over which period counties have carried rising costs through property taxes.
